## About Ghanex Tech

Ghanex Tech is a software development company working with businesses and teams that need technology shaped around their real requirements.

Sometimes that means building a custom CRM or ERP. Sometimes it means connecting systems that do not talk to each other, automating a time-consuming process, or taking an early product idea and turning it into a working application. We start by understanding the problem, then work out what should be built and which technology makes sense.

We work across **custom software development, business applications, AI and machine learning, LLM-based solutions, automation, cloud platforms, and product engineering**. Our goal is straightforward: build useful software, make it dependable, and leave room for it to grow.

## What We Work On

### Custom software and product development

We develop applications around the way a business operates, rather than asking the business to change its process to fit an off-the-shelf tool.

- Custom web and mobile applications
- SaaS platforms and digital products
- Business-specific applications and portals
- Backend systems, APIs, and third-party integrations
- Improvement and modernization of existing software

### CRM, ERP, and business systems

We help teams bring their customer information, operations, and everyday workflows into systems that fit their needs.

- Custom Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems
- Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) solutions
- Lead, customer, sales, and operational workflows
- Internal dashboards and business applications
- Integrations between existing tools and platforms

The features and scope are decided with each client. We do not assume that every business needs the same system.

### AI, machine learning, and LLMs

We are interested in AI that has a clear purpose. That could be helping people find information, assisting with a repetitive task, or adding language-model capabilities to an existing product.

- Generative AI applications
- Large Language Model (LLM) integrations
- AI assistants and task-focused agents
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications
- Machine learning experiments and practical use cases
- Open-source model exploration and evaluation

### Automation and integrations

A lot of time is lost when people have to repeat the same work or move information manually between tools. We build integrations and automated workflows to reduce that friction.

- Business process and workflow automation
- API development and integrations
- AI-assisted workflows
- Data synchronisation between systems
- Automation of routine operational tasks

### Cloud and engineering

We take the practical needs of running software into account, not just the first release.

- Cloud-ready application architecture
- Deployment and release support
- Database and backend engineering
- Performance, reliability, and maintainability improvements
- Ongoing enhancements and technical support

## What You Can Expect From Our Work

We do not measure a good delivery by the number of features or lines of code. We look at whether the solution addresses the problem it was meant to solve.

- **A clear understanding of the requirement.** We take time to understand the users, current process, constraints, and expected outcome.
- **A suitable technical approach.** We choose tools and architecture based on the project, not simply because a technology is popular.
- **Useful, focused functionality.** We keep the product centred on what people need and avoid adding complexity without a reason.
- **Maintainable code.** We aim for clean structure, sensible documentation, and software that can be understood and improved.
- **Thoughtful integrations.** We help systems share information and fit into the wider workflow.
- **Testing and review.** We check the important user journeys, fix issues, and use feedback to improve the result.
- **Communication throughout.** We keep decisions, progress, open questions, and next steps visible.

## How We Work

Every project is different, but our process usually follows these steps:

| Stage | What we do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **01 — Understand** | Discuss the problem, users, existing process, goals, and constraints. |
| **02 — Plan** | Agree on the scope, priorities, milestones, and what a successful result should look like. |
| **03 — Design** | Map the user experience, architecture, data, and integrations. |
| **04 — Build** | Develop in manageable stages so progress can be reviewed and tested. |
| **05 — Test** | Validate important workflows, address issues, and incorporate feedback. |
| **06 — Release** | Prepare the application and support its deployment. |
| **07 — Improve** | Learn from real usage and plan fixes, maintenance, and future enhancements. |

We prefer regular feedback over waiting until the very end to show the work. It helps everyone stay aligned and gives us a chance to catch misunderstandings early.

## Open Source: Build With Us

We want Ghanex Tech to be more than a place where software gets delivered to clients. We also want it to be a place where people can share knowledge, experiment, and build useful things together.

**We are happy to receive contributions and proposed changes to our open-source projects.** Developers, students, researchers, and independent builders are welcome to explore our repositories, report issues, improve documentation, suggest features, and submit pull requests.

A contribution does not have to be a large feature. A clear bug report, a useful test, a documentation fix, or a thoughtful suggestion can make a project better.

### Have an idea for a product?

This is something we are especially interested in. If you have an idea for a tool, application, or open-source product that could solve a real-world problem, tell us about it.

You do not need to arrive with a finished prototype or a complete business plan. Explain the problem, who it affects, and what you think could make things better. We would be glad to hear you out and explore the idea with you.

We are particularly keen on **building free tools for real-world applications**: practical resources that people, communities, developers, and businesses can use without unnecessary barriers.

### Areas we'd love to collaborate on

- Free and open tools that address everyday problems
- AI and machine learning applications with a practical use
- LLM-based tools, model evaluation, and useful experiments
- Workflow automation and system integrations
- Developer utilities, libraries, and APIs
- Small product ideas that could grow through community input

### How to contribute

1. Browse the project's README, issues, and existing discussions.
2. For a significant change, open an issue or discussion first so we can agree on the direction.
3. Fork the repository and create a focused branch for your work.
4. Make your changes and add tests or documentation where they are useful.
5. Open a pull request explaining what changed and why.

Please check the contribution guide and licence in the specific repository. Each project may have its own process.

## What We're Exploring Next

Our interests will keep developing as we learn from projects, contributors, and the problems people bring to us. Some areas we want to spend more time on are:

- **Open-source products:** releasing useful tools that people can use, adapt, and improve together.
- **AI and machine learning:** exploring practical applications, model evaluation, and responsible implementation.
- **LLMs and AI agents:** experimenting with open models, RAG, fine-tuning, and task-focused assistants.
- **Automation:** making repetitive work simpler for individuals and organisations.
- **Developer tools:** creating utilities and resources that make building and maintaining software easier.
- **Community-led projects:** working with people who understand a problem and want to help build a solution.

These are the directions we are interested in pursuing, rather than promises of products with fixed release dates. We would rather build something useful with the right people than publish something just to meet a deadline.
